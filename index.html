    ❓ How to play

    • Goal: be the first team to reach the target score (5, 11 or 15 – your choice in the menu).
    • Serve scoring: only the serving team scores. If the other team wins the rally, it is a side-out and they serve next.
    • A rally ends when the ball touches the sand on one side. The team on the other side wins it.
    • Three touches: each team may hit the ball up to 3 times in a row. A 4th touch is a fault.
    • Serve: the ball hangs in the air for a moment and then drops by itself – but you can hit it right away.
    • Modes: 1 : 1 on one keyboard, 2 : 2 with two humans against two CPU heroes, or 1 : 1 against the CPU (Easy / Normal / Hard).
    • Phone: play sideways. Use the buttons at the bottom: ◀ ▶ to move, ▲ to jump, ⚡ for power-ups, ⏸ for the menu. Add the game to your home screen (Install) for full screen.
    • Figures: before each match pick your name, hero (Pixel Hero, Cyborg, Cowboy, Ninja, Kebabi the doner kebab, Adventurer, Santa, McRib the burger, Transformer the car, Cloudy the thundercloud, Gagari the astronaut or Kindi the chocolate bar) and colour (swimwear, paint or wrapper).
    • Stages: pick the arena in the menu (or Random). Beach is the classic. Moon Station has low gravity – long, floaty jumps and rallies. Underwater is slow and heavy: everything moves more slowly and the ball drifts. Döner Shop has the same physics as the beach – the net is a rotating doner spit and a chef slices away next to it. Black Hole Arena has a black hole above the net that gently pulls the ball towards it. In the Volley Throne the first five rounds use random stages and the final is fought in the Black Hole Arena.
    • Online: one player creates a room and shares the 4-letter code (or the invite link), the other joins. The host's browser runs the game, so the host should keep the tab in the foreground.
    • Power-ups: every 30 s each player gets one falling from the sky. Catch it with your hero (3 can be stored), then press Ctrl to use it. See the Power-ups page for what they do.
    • Volley Throne: a single-player climb in Volley Fight rules. You face 6 random heroes one after another, each CPU stronger than the last (first to 5 points, the final first to 7). Lose a fight and you start again from the bottom – win all six and you claim the throne.
    • Volley Fight: choose “Volley Fight” under Rules. Every hero has a weapon – two kinds. Melee – press the special key (Ctrl / ⚡) to swing; the weapon aims at the ball by itself and, if the ball is in reach, smashes it away faster than a normal hit (one of your 3 touches). Each weapon differs: Pixel Hero's sword is the all-rounder, Kebabi's doner knife and the Ninja's katana are the fastest and flattest with a short recharge, the Adventurer's lasso reaches very far and lobs the ball high, Santa's birch rod makes the ball dip steeply behind the net, Kindi's baseball bat hits as flat and fast as the katana, and the Transformer kicks the ball up and forward from below. Ranged (Cyborg shotgun: 3 pellets, slow to reload · Cowboy revolver: 1 bullet, fires again quickly · McRib's gherkin cannon: slices that bend the ball · Gagari's black hole cannon: a hit makes the ball vanish and drop from the sky at a random spot on the opponents' side): the weapon auto-aims at the ball: press the special key to fire at it (Cloudy: lightning in all directions). Shots fly over the net. A hit speeds up a ball that is flying to your opponents and slows down one that is coming at you; shots are not touches. Cloudy is the exception: no aiming – one key press sends lightning bolts in all directions at once, and they bend the ball. Weapons need time to recharge (bar under the name). There are no power-ups in this mode.
    • High scores: wins against the CPU count – points = lead × 100 × level factor + longest rally × 10.
