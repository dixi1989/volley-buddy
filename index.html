    ⚡ Power-ups

    Every 30 s each player gets one falling from the sky – catch it (max. 3 stored), then press Ctrl to use it.

    ⌨ Controls

    Player 1 AD move · W jump · Ctrl use power-up
    Player 2 ←→ move · ↑ jump · Right Ctrl use power-up
    1 vs CPU WASD or arrow keys
    Shift works instead of Ctrl (Ctrl+W closes the browser tab!).
    P / Esc pause · M all sound · N music · F fullscreen · 1 2 3 pick a mode in the menu

    ❓ How to play

    • Goal: be the first team to reach the target score (5, 11 or 15 – your choice in the menu).
    • Serve scoring: only the serving team scores. If the other team wins the rally, it is a side-out and they serve next.
    • A rally ends when the ball touches the sand on one side. The team on the other side wins it.
    • Three touches: each team may hit the ball up to 3 times in a row. A 4th touch is a fault.
    • Serve: the ball hangs in the air for a moment and then drops by itself – but you can hit it right away.
    • Modes: 1 : 1 on one keyboard, 2 : 2 with two humans against two CPU bears, or 1 : 1 against the CPU (Easy / Normal / Hard).
    • Power-ups: every 30 s each player gets one falling from the sky. Catch it with your bear (3 can be stored), then press Ctrl to use it. See the Power-ups page for what they do.
    • High scores: wins against the CPU count – points = lead × 100 × level factor + longest rally × 10.
